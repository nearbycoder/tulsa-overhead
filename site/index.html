The story

Six hours out.
Forty grand in debt.

Polly’s barely home on parole when Mama June calls in a favor. Cousin Dewey borrowed forty thousand from the Gusher Syndicate. Now they want his tire shop in Broken Arrow.

Pick up a ride in the Rose District and follow the money. Eight chapters take you down Route 66, onto the docks at Catoosa and into downtown Tulsa, where old oil money still owns the block.
